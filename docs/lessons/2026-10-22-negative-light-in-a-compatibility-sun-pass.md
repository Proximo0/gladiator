What happened: To fade the foot contact blobs inside the sun shadow, the sand's light() subtracted the blob's ambient share from DIFFUSE_LIGHT (times ATTENUATION). In full sun the blob came out 15-35% too light whatever factor was used: Compatibility draws the shadowed sun in an additive pass of its own (tonemapped and clamped there), so light() can only scale or add the sun's own light, never take away ambient from the base pass
Rule: In a Compatibility light(), only scale or add a light's own contribution; split an effect that must depend on the shadow into a share on the base pass (EMISSION) and a scale on the light, and measure it on a fixed bench (tests/render/contact_sun_check) under the arena's lights
