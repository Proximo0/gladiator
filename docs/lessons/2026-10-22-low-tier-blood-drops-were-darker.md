What happened: Low-tier blood drops were darker than the medium/high impostors (mean red 132 vs 146): the teardrop mesh was wound counter-clockwise, so `cull_back` dropped the near half and drew the far half, whose normals face away (Godot flips back-face normals too, so `cull_front` looked the same). Before the impostors every tier used that mesh, so tier-vs-tier shots matched and hid it
Rule: Debug a shading difference by writing `NORMAL * 0.5 + 0.5` to EMISSION and sampling the centre pixel (blue = facing the camera); check procedural mesh winding against Godot's own SphereMesh in a unit test
