What happened: CPU thrusts aimed at plate gaps (neck, face, elbows, knees) left the share of plate hits that found a gap unchanged (63% before, 61% after over 48 fights): the 7 cm joint bands already cover about 40% of each limb and the torso's top band, so most plate hits were gap hits whatever the aim. A hit-and-run retreat for the lighter fighter (sprint out after every blow) made it lose more (iron v naked 60% to 73-75%): backing straight out of a strike takes the riposte with no guard
Rule: Before teaching the AI to exploit a mechanic, measure how often it already pays off (here gap hits per plate hit in the bot hitlog); and measure each new behaviour alone (on/off variants on the same seeds) before keeping it
