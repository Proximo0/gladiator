What happened: Textured bronze read dull and steel showed patches, and tuning `roughness`/`metallic` in the library materials changed nothing: Godot's `ORMMaterial3D` shader takes ROUGHNESS and METALLIC straight from the ORM map and ignores both values, so every metal rendered at roughness ~0.9 and metallic 1 (and FighterArt's armor metallic cap was a no-op)
Rule: Feed an ORM map through a StandardMaterial3D's roughness/metallic/AO textures with channel selects (G, B, R) so the factors multiply; when a material change shows no pixel difference in a capture, check the generated shader uses the value before tuning further
